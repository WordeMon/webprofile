Individual Research Project

In my dual credit informatics class, I had the opportunity to choose 2 underrepresented innovators to showcase and research about. I choose Shafi Goldwasser and Clearance "Skip" Ellis. For this project, I focused on their contributions to the field of informatics and how their work has influenced modern technology. I also examined the challenges they faced and the impact of their innovations. For the second part of the project, I had to develop two new business ventures based on technology. I chose one advanced topic to explore in depth and one simple topic to provide for a high school student level. In conclusion, this project allowed me to learn about underrepresented voices in technology and the importance of diversity in innovation.
